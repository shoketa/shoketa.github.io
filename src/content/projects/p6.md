## Game pitch

"You are a spacefaring astronaut who has crashed on an alien planet. Travel around to find your missing crewmates, gather engine scraps to restore your spaceship and escape!"

## My responsibilities

My main responsibilities for this project shifted slightly. As well as VFX and shaders, I also started creating Houdini tools for our level designers, and went deeper into the lower-level graphics pipeline to improve it.
