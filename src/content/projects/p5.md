Project time: 14 weeks, half-time · Team size: 21

## Game pitch

"As an enraged spirit, take revenge upon the god who caused the death of a beloved one. Fight enemies and dash your way up the mountain as a golden corruption threatens to take over your home."

## My responsibilities

My main responsibilities for this project were mostly shaders and VFX to make the world feel alive. I also created a tool for creating splatmaps in Unreal with the use of render targets.
